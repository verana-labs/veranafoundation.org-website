# **SECTION 2**

NATURE OF AGREEMENT; HYBRID BINDING STRUCTURE

**(Binding in Part Immediately Upon Execution; Deferred Binding in Part Until Ratification)**

2.1 **Immediate Binding Provisions Upon Execution**.

Notwithstanding that the Verana Foundation has not yet been incorporated as a separate legal entity and that its jurisdiction of incorporation remains to be determined by the organizers, the Parties expressly acknowledge, covenant, and agree that the following provisions of this Agreement (the “Immediate Binding Provisions”) shall be legally binding, operative, and enforceable as of the Effective Date, without need for further action, approval, or ratification by any future Verana Foundation entity: Section 4 (Intellectual Property and Licensing); Section 5 (Confidentiality); Section 6 (Code of Conduct and Participation Requirements); Section 7 (Membership Fees); Section 8.5(e) (Survival of Licensing Commitments Upon Termination); Section 9 (Limitation of Liability); Section 10 (Governing Law); Section 11 (Dispute Resolution); and Section 12 (Miscellaneous). The Member expressly agrees that these Immediate Binding Provisions shall bind the Member vis-à-vis the Foundation (in Formation) and shall continue to bind the Member vis-à-vis the Verana Foundation once incorporated, regardless of the jurisdiction ultimately selected for incorporation.

2.2 **Deferred Binding Provisions Activated Upon Ratification**.

All other provisions of this Agreement (the “Deferred Binding Provisions”), including but not limited to Section 1 (Definitions), Section 3 (Membership Rights and Obligations), and Section 8 (Term, Suspension, and Termination), shall not become legally binding or enforceable upon the Member until such time as the Verana Foundation is formally incorporated in whatever jurisdiction the organizers shall determine and the incorporated Verana Foundation ratifies this Agreement in accordance with Section 2.3. Until such ratification, the Deferred Binding Provisions shall remain inoperative and shall confer no rights or obligations upon any Party.

2.3 **Ratification Upon Incorporation of the Foundation**.

Upon incorporation of the Verana Foundation in the jurisdiction selected by its organizers (which jurisdiction need not be predetermined, disclosed, or mutually agreed as a condition of Member execution of this Agreement), the Foundation may, by resolution of its duly constituted governing body, elect to ratify and adopt this Agreement. Upon issuance of written notice of such ratification to the Member, all Deferred Binding Provisions shall, without need for any additional signature, amendment, consent, or action by the Member, immediately become binding, enforceable, and fully operative as between the Member and the Foundation.

2.4 **Legal Effect Prior to Ratification**.

Prior to the incorporation and ratification of this Agreement by the Foundation:

(a) the Immediate Binding Provisions shall remain legally binding as between the Member and the Foundation (in Formation), and thereafter automatically upon the incorporated Foundation;

(b) the Deferred Binding Provisions shall be without legal force or effect and shall not impose any obligations upon, or create any rights in favor of, either Party; and

(c) the Member shall not be entitled, as a matter of enforceable right, to the membership rights, privileges, participatory entitlements, or benefits contemplated by the Deferred Binding Provisions; provided, however, that the Foundation (in Formation) may admit the Member on a provisional basis and grant the Member participation in Working Groups and other Foundation activities during the formation period. Any such provisional participation (i) shall be governed by and subject to the Immediate Binding Provisions, including without limitation the intellectual property and licensing obligations of Section 4, the confidentiality obligations of Section 5, the Code of Conduct of Section 6, and the fee obligations of Section 7; (ii) may be modified, suspended, or revoked by the Foundation (in Formation) at any time in its sole discretion; and (iii) shall not constitute or give rise to any vested or enforceable membership right prior to ratification.

2.5 **Continuation After Ratification**.

Upon ratification of this Agreement by the incorporated Foundation, the Immediate Binding Provisions and the Deferred Binding Provisions shall merge and operate henceforth as a single, unified contractual framework governing the Member’s participation. From such time forward, the Member shall be entitled to the rights and subject to the obligations corresponding to its selected Membership Class, without prejudice to any continuing obligations under the Immediate Binding Provisions.

2.6 **Mutual Acknowledgment Regarding Uncertain Incorporation Jurisdiction**.

The Parties expressly acknowledge, agree, and intend that:

(a) the jurisdiction of incorporation of the Verana Foundation has not yet been determined and may be selected at the discretion of the organizers;

(b) the Member’s execution of this Agreement constitutes advance consent to the eventual incorporation of the Foundation in any jurisdiction chosen by the organizers, provided that the Foundation shall provide written notice to the Member upon incorporation;

(c) the validity, enforceability, and operative effect of the Immediate Binding Provisions shall not be impaired, delayed, limited, suspended, or otherwise affected by the absence of a predetermined incorporation jurisdiction; and

(d) the Member shall not assert, claim, or allege that uncertainty regarding the Foundation’s future legal domicile invalidates, delays, or limits the binding effect of the Immediate Binding Provisions.

2.7 **No Right of Member to Condition Ratification**.

The Member acknowledges and agrees that it shall have no right, power, claim, or standing to impose any condition, restriction, limitation, or prerequisite upon the Foundation’s incorporation or upon the subsequent ratification of this Agreement. Ratification is an internal governance act of the Foundation and shall not require Member approval or consent.

2.8 **Survival**.

All provisions of this Section 2 shall survive suspension, expiration, or termination of this Agreement and shall remain in full force and effect to the maximum extent permissible under applicable law.

# **ANNEX C**

## **CODE OF CONDUCT**

**(Binding Immediately Upon Execution Pursuant to Section 2.1 of the Membership Agreement)**

C.1 **Purpose and Applicability.**

This **Code of Conduct** (this **“Code”**) establishes mandatory standards of professional behavior, ethical engagement, and community interaction applicable to all Members of the Foundation, all Member Representatives, and all other individuals participating in activities, programs, initiatives, Working Groups, committees, or collaboration environments operated, sponsored, sanctioned, or recognized by the Verana Foundation (in Formation), and upon incorporation, the Verana Foundation (collectively, the **“Foundation”**). This Code is binding upon the Member and all Member Representatives immediately upon execution of the Membership Agreement and shall remain binding thereafter to the fullest extent permissible under applicable law.

C.2 **Professional Conduct and Integrity.**

Each Member and Member Representative shall, at all times, conduct themselves in a professional, courteous, honest, and respectful manner. Conduct that is abusive, hostile, aggressive, harassing, deceptive, disruptive, or otherwise inconsistent with the Foundation’s mission, principles, or community expectations is strictly prohibited.

C.3 **Respectful Communications.**

All communications in Foundation-related environments, whether written, verbal, or electronic, shall be conducted respectfully and constructively. Disparagement, ridicule, defamation, personal attacks, inflammatory speech, or communication intended to demean, intimidate, or silence any participant shall not be permitted.

C.4 **Non-Discrimination and Inclusiveness.**

No Member or Member Representative shall engage in discrimination, harassment, or unequal treatment based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any other protected characteristic. The Foundation is committed to maintaining an inclusive environment for all participants.

C.5 **Collaboration and Participation Standards.**

Members participating in any Working Group, Task Force, committee, or collaborative forum shall engage constructively, refrain from obstructive or disruptive behavior, and comply fully with all applicable Working Group charters, participation rules, contribution processes, security protocols, and governance procedures adopted by the Foundation.

C.6 **Conduct Relating to Contributions.**

All Contributions submitted by Members or Member Representatives must:

(a) be accurate, non-deceptive, and technically sound to the best of the contributor’s knowledge;

(b) not contain malicious code, harmful logic, concealed functionality, or manipulative structures;

(c) comply with all licensing obligations set forth in Section 4 of the Membership Agreement;

(d) not infringe or knowingly violate any intellectual property rights of third parties; and

(e) not introduce proprietary, confidential, or encumbered material inconsistent with Foundation policies.

C.7 **Conflicts of Interest; Disclosure Obligations.**

Members and Member Representatives shall avoid actual, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest. Members shall timely disclose to the Foundation any personal, professional, commercial, or organizational relationships that may reasonably influence their contributions, participation, or decision-making within any Foundation context.

C.8 **Confidentiality and Data Protection.**

Members and Member Representatives shall strictly comply with the confidentiality obligations described in Section 5 of the Membership Agreement. Members shall handle any confidential information or sensitive data received in the course of Foundation activities with the utmost care and shall not disclose such information except as expressly permitted under the Membership Agreement or required by applicable law.

C.9 **Prohibited Conduct.**

Without limiting the generality of this Code, the following conduct is strictly prohibited:

(a) harassment, intimidation, or threats of any kind;

(b) intentional disruption or manipulation of Foundation processes, Working Groups, repository workflows, or governance functions;

(c) unauthorized access to Foundation systems, repositories, documents, or communication channels;

(d) misrepresentation of identity, affiliation, authority, or membership status;

(e) failure to disclose known intellectual property restrictions, encumbrances, or rights pertinent to Contributions;

(f) submission of Contributions designed to exploit, compromise, undermine, or attack the Verana Network or any Foundation Artifact;

(g) use of Foundation participation to promote unrelated commercial, political, or ideological agendas inconsistent with Foundation purposes;

(h) any conduct contrary to applicable law or to the integrity, neutrality, safety, or mission of the Foundation.

C.10 **Reporting Violations.**

The Foundation (in Formation), and upon incorporation, the Foundation, shall establish mechanisms for reporting alleged violations of this Code. Members and Member Representatives shall cooperate fully with any Foundation inquiry, investigation, or review relating to alleged misconduct.

C.11 **Enforcement and Remedies.**

The Foundation shall have sole and absolute discretion to enforce this Code. Upon determining that a violation has occurred, the Foundation may impose any sanction it deems appropriate, including but not limited to:

(a) verbal or written warnings;

(b) mandatory corrective or remedial actions;

(c) removal of the Member or Member Representative from any Working Group, Task Force, or Foundation activity;

(d) suspension of membership rights;

(e) termination of membership pursuant to Section 8 of the Membership Agreement;

(f) permanent exclusion from Foundation activities; or

(g) any other disciplinary action deemed necessary to safeguard the integrity of the Foundation and its mission.

The Foundation shall have no liability to the Member or to any Member Representative for any enforcement action taken in good faith pursuant to this Annex C.

C.12 **No Appeal as of Right.**

Members shall have no automatic right to appeal any sanction imposed under this Code unless the Foundation adopts an appeals mechanism by written policy. Any appeal that is permitted shall not suspend or delay the effectiveness of the sanction unless expressly authorized by the Foundation.

C.13 **Survival.**

All obligations stated in this Annex C that, by their nature, are intended to survive termination—including but not limited to conduct obligations, confidentiality obligations, and restrictions relating to Contributions—shall survive the termination or suspension of membership and shall remain in full force and effect in accordance with their terms.
