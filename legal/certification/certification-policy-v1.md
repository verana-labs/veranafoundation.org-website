# **VERANA FOUNDATION**

CERTIFICATION POLICY v1 DRAFT 20261012

### **VERANA CERTIFICATION PROGRAM**

**Status.** Draft for review by the Certification Office and counsel. Not in force. This document becomes effective when 2060 OÜ publishes it as version 1 on veranafoundation.org and anchors its digest as the governance framework of the Verana Certification Ecosystem.

**Controller.** Until the Verana Foundation is incorporated and accepts assignment of the Program in writing, 2060 OÜ (Estonia, registry number 16853041) operates the Program in its own name as organizer of the Verana Foundation, and is the controller. Until that acceptance, the **"Foundation"** means 2060 OÜ. From that acceptance, the **"Foundation"** means the Verana Foundation, which becomes the controller.

---

## SECTION 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This Certification Policy (the **"Policy"**) governs the Verana Certification Program (the **"Program"**), through which the Foundation certifies the competence of individuals and organizations that use, sell, build or operate products and services based on the Verana specifications and software.

1.2 The Policy binds the Foundation, every Authorized Certificator, every candidate and every holder of a certification. It is incorporated by reference into the Authorized Certificator Agreement and into the Candidate Terms.

1.3 The Policy is also the governance framework document of the **Verana Certification Ecosystem**, the Ecosystem of the Verana Public Registry through which certifications are issued as verifiable credentials (Section 7).

1.4 Nothing in the Policy grants membership, governance, voting or validator rights in the Foundation, the Verana Council or the Verana Network. Membership is governed by the Membership Agreement.

## SECTION 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 **"Authorized Certificator"** or **"Certificator"** means a holder of a Qualifying Membership as an Associate, accredited under Section 10 to deliver training, assess candidates and issue certifications under the Program.

2.2 **"Candidate"** means an individual enrolled for a certification, or an organization applying for a designation.

2.3 **"Certification Office"** or **"Office"** means the function of the Foundation that administers the Program, exercised by the Foundation's staff.

2.4 **"Certified Instructor"** means an employee or contractor of a Certificator who meets the requirements of Section 4.5 and is recorded as such by the Office.

2.5 **"Certified Organization"** means an organization holding a valid organization designation under Section 9.

2.6 **"Certified Professional"** means an individual holding a valid certification under Section 4.

2.7 **"Curriculum"** means the modules, tracks, learning material, labs, assessments and Assessment Criteria published by the Foundation under the Program, in the version applicable at the time of assessment. The Curriculum does not include the Marker Pack.

2.8 **"Designation"** means one of the organization certifications of Section 9.

2.9 **"Marks"** means the names and logos "Verana Certified Professional", "Verana Certified Associate", "Verana Certified Expert", "Verana Certified Instructor", "Verana Certified Integrator", "Verana Certified Solution Provider", "Verana Certified Operator", "Verana Authorized Certificator" and "Verana Certification Program", and any mark the Foundation later designates as a Program mark.

2.10 **"Module"** means a unit of the Curriculum with defined learning objectives and one assessment.

2.11 **"Program Version"** means a published version of the Curriculum, aligned with a version of the Verana specifications.

2.12 **"Registry"** means the public record of certifications maintained by the Foundation, as the participant tree of the Verana Certification Ecosystem and as the registry pages of veranafoundation.org.

2.13 **"Showcase and Certification Working Group"** or **"Working Group"** means the working group of the Foundation that authors and maintains the Curriculum.

2.14 **"Track"** means a role-based set of Modules whose completion earns a certification.

2.15 Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Membership Agreement.

2.16 **"Qualifying Membership"** means a membership of the class named where this term is used, Contributor or Associate, that is in force under the Membership Agreement. Until the incorporated Verana Foundation has ratified that Membership Agreement, provisional participation of that class which 2060 OÜ has accepted and has not revoked is a Qualifying Membership.

2.17 **"Assessment Criteria"** means the statement, published with the Curriculum for a Module or a Designation, of what is judged, how the points are allocated and what the pass mark is, together with the candidate-facing rules of the assessment, including its duration, the materials allowed and the assistance forbidden. Assessment Criteria do not include a model answer, an expected configuration or a lab solution. A statement that gives the answer is part of the Marker Pack.

2.18 **"Marker Pack"** means the question banks, model answers, point-by-point marking notes, lab solutions and the proctoring material that describes how a breach of Section 5.6 is detected, together with any other material the Foundation designates as part of the Marker Pack. The Marker Pack is confidential. It is made available only to the Foundation and to the Certified Instructors and staff who need it to proctor or grade.

## SECTION 3. GOVERNANCE

3.1 **The Office** approves Certificators, Program Versions, assessment instruments and this Policy; maintains the Registry; decides appeals at first instance; conducts audits; and issues the credentials of Section 7 that the Foundation issues itself.

3.2 **The Working Group** proposes the Curriculum and its versions, reviews training material and assessment instruments, and records its decisions in its published minutes. The Marker Pack is confidential and is not published in the minutes. The Assessment Criteria are published with the Curriculum.

3.3 **The Foundation** approves the fee schedule of Annex A, the Authorized Certificator Agreement and the Candidate Terms.

3.4 Decisions of the Office are recorded in the Foundation's audit log. A decision that affects a Candidate, a holder or a Certificator is notified in writing.

## SECTION 4. INDIVIDUAL CERTIFICATIONS

4.1 **Levels.** The Program has three levels:

| Level | Name | How it is obtained |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verana Certified Associate (Fundamentals) | Self-study of the published material and an online knowledge exam administered by the Foundation. No Certificator is involved. |
| 2 | Verana Certified Professional, in a Track | Completion of the Track's Modules: a proctored knowledge exam administered by the Foundation and a practical lab or case study graded by a Certificator against the Assessment Criteria. Workshops are optional and offered by Certificators. |
| 3 | Verana Certified Expert, in a Track | Level 2 in the Track held for at least one year, a portfolio of real deployments or engagements, and an oral defense before two Certified Instructors from two different Certificators. Level 3 opens with a later Program Version. |

4.2 **Tracks and Modules.** The Tracks, their Modules and the core Modules common to every Track are published in the Curriculum. A Module passed under a Program Version counts for every Track that includes it under the same Program Version.

4.3 **Eligibility.** Any individual of legal age who holds a Qualifying Membership as a Contributor may enroll. Contributor membership is free for individuals and is obtained on veranafoundation.org. The Qualifying Membership shall be active at each assessment and at the issuance of a certification; a lapse does not affect a certification already issued. Certificators may add commercial conditions to their workshops but not to the assessment itself beyond the fees of Annex A.

4.4 **Enrollment.** Candidates for Level 1 enroll on veranafoundation.org. Candidates for Level 2 and Level 3 enroll through a Certificator, which onboards them to the Program and records their enrollment with the Office. Every Candidate accepts the Candidate Terms before the first assessment.

4.5 **Certified Instructors.** A Certified Instructor holds Level 2 in the Track taught, has passed the instructor Module of the Curriculum, has delivered one observed session to the Office's satisfaction, and works for a Certificator. The Office may withdraw the instructor status for cause.

## SECTION 5. ASSESSMENT

5.1 **Knowledge exams** are administered on the Foundation platform, drawn at random from the question bank in the Marker Pack of the applicable Program Version, and graded automatically. Unless the Assessment Criteria state otherwise for a Module, an exam lasts sixty minutes and the pass mark is seventy percent of the available points. Level 2 and Level 3 exams are proctored by a Certified Instructor; Level 1 exams are not proctored.

5.2 **Practical labs** are executed on the environments designated by the Curriculum, which may include the Verana Playground and the Verana test networks. Where the Curriculum provides automated checks, their result is binding; a Certified Instructor reviews the remaining criteria against the Assessment Criteria.

5.3 **Case studies, live demonstrations and oral defenses** are graded by a Certified Instructor against the Assessment Criteria. The Office second-marks a sample of graded work each quarter.

5.4 **Retakes.** A Candidate who fails an assessment may retake it twice, with at least fourteen days between attempts. A third failure requires a new enrollment after ninety days.

5.5 **Accommodations.** A Candidate may request additional time or another reasonable accommodation from the Office before the assessment. Requests are granted where justified and recorded.

5.6 **Integrity.** A Candidate shall not copy, record, share or attempt to obtain the Marker Pack, impersonate another Candidate or be impersonated, or receive assistance during an assessment beyond what the Curriculum allows. A breach voids the attempt, may void existing certifications of the Candidate and may bar the Candidate from the Program. A Certificator that facilitates a breach is subject to Section 10.8.

5.7 **Language.** Assessments are available in English. Other languages are added by Program Version; the English version prevails in case of discrepancy.

## SECTION 6. VALIDITY, RENEWAL AND CONTINUING EDUCATION

6.1 An individual certification is valid for two years from its issuance date.

6.2 **Renewal.** A certification is renewed by passing the knowledge exam of the Track under the current Program Version before expiry. A certification that expires can be obtained again as a new certification.

6.3 **Delta Modules.** When a Program Version follows a major version of the Verana specifications, the Curriculum publishes a delta Module. Holders keep their certification until its expiry; holders who pass the delta Module carry the new Program Version on their certification.

6.4 **Continuing education.** A holder who documents participation in a working group of the Foundation during at least twelve months of the validity period is exempt from the renewal exam fee of Annex A. The renewal exam itself remains required.

6.5 Program Versions and the dates from which each applies to new assessments are published on veranafoundation.org.

## SECTION 7. ISSUANCE, THE REGISTRY AND PUBLICATION

7.1 **Verifiable credentials.** Each certification is issued as a verifiable credential under the Verana Certification Ecosystem:

| Credential | Issued by | Held by |
|---|---|---|
| Verana Certified Associate (Level 1) | The Foundation | The individual, in a personal wallet |
| Verana Certified Professional (Level 2) | The Certificator that enrolled the Candidate | The individual, in a personal wallet |
| Verana Certified Organization | The Certificator that reviewed the organization | The organization, published on its DID |
| Verana Authorized Certificator | The Foundation | The Certificator, published on its DID |

The Foundation issues a Verana Certified Associate credential when the Candidate passes the Level 1 exam and any exam fee is paid. The Foundation issues a Verana Authorized Certificator credential on accreditation under Section 10.2. A Verana Certified Expert (Level 3) credential is not issued under this version. The Program Version that opens Level 3 names its issuer.

Where Section 10.3 or Section 10.6 provides that the Office certifies, the Foundation is the issuer of that credential. It stands in the place of the Certificator for issuance, for revocation under Section 8, and for re-issue under Section 7.3.

For a Verana Certified Professional credential or a Verana Certified Organization credential that a Certificator issues, the Certificator reports the result to the Office within five business days. The Foundation records that reported result within ten business days after receiving it, unless within those ten days it notifies the Certificator that it has opened a review under Section 10.5. If it does not open a review, the reported result stands. The Certificator issues the credential within five business days after the Foundation records that the requirements of that credential are met, and does not issue a result the Foundation has overturned. After issuance, a challenge is brought under Section 8.

7.2 Credentials carry the name of the holder, the Track or Designation, the level, the Program Version, the certificate identifier, the issuance and expiry dates, and the issuer. The issuer is the Foundation or the Certificator, named as such. A credential the Foundation re-issues under Section 7.3 also names the original Certificator. The credential schemas are published by the Foundation.

7.3 **Network.** Until the Verana main network is available for the Program, credentials are issued on a Verana test network and the Registry pages of veranafoundation.org are the authoritative record. When the main network becomes available, the Foundation re-creates the Verana Certification Ecosystem there. The issuer re-issues its own valid credentials at no charge, keeps the certificate identifier, and does so within the period the Foundation sets. If a Certificator does not re-issue, or its accreditation has ended, the Foundation re-issues that credential, names itself as issuer, and records the original Certificator.

7.4 **Registry.** Certified Organizations and Certificators are listed in the Registry. Certified Professionals are listed only with their consent, given in the Candidate Terms and revocable at any time. The Registry shows the holder's name, the certification, the Program Version, the validity dates and the issuer. Where the Foundation has re-issued a credential under Section 7.3, the Registry also shows the original Certificator.

7.5 **Verification.** Anyone may verify a certification on veranafoundation.org by its certificate identifier or by presenting the credential, and through trust resolution of the holder's DID for organizations.

7.6 A certificate identifier is unique and never reused. A re-issued credential keeps its certificate identifier.

## SECTION 8. SUSPENSION AND REVOCATION OF CERTIFICATIONS

8.1 The Office may suspend or revoke a certification when it was obtained in breach of Section 5.6, when the holder misuses the Marks, when a Certified Organization no longer meets Section 9.3, or when the holder brings the Program into disrepute.

8.2 The holder is notified with the reasons and may appeal under Section 12. A revoked certification is removed from the Registry. The issuer sets the credential status to revoked within five business days of the Office's instruction. If the issuer does not do so, or is a Certificator that no longer holds an accreditation, the Foundation records the credential as revoked in the Registry, and a verifier treats that recorded status as the current status of the credential.

8.3 Certifications issued by a Certificator whose accreditation ends remain valid until their expiry unless revoked under Section 8.1. Re-issue of those certifications is governed by Section 7.3.

## SECTION 9. ORGANIZATION DESIGNATIONS

9.1 **Designations.** The Program certifies organizations under three Designations:

| Designation | For organizations that | Certified staff required | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verana Certified Integrator | Build products and services on Verana | Two Certified Professionals in the Developer and Integrator Track; one in the Infrastructure Engineer or the Solutions Consultant Track | A verifiable service operated by the organization, live on a Verana network and passing the conformance checks designated by the Curriculum; a documented support process |
| Verana Certified Solution Provider | Sell, resell or advise on Verana-based solutions | One Certified Professional in each of the Solutions Consultant and Pre-sales Tracks; one in the Legal and Compliance or the Procurement and Tenders Track | Two customer references, or one documented engagement reviewed by the Certificator |
| Verana Certified Operator | Run their own ecosystem or verifiable services on Verana | One Certified Professional in each of the Ecosystem Operator and Infrastructure Engineer Tracks | A live Ecosystem or verifiable service operated by the organization and a published governance framework |

9.2 **Membership.** A Certified Organization shall hold a Qualifying Membership as a Contributor, active for the whole validity period of its Designation.

9.3 **Process.** The organization applies through a Certificator, which verifies the staff requirements against the Registry, reviews the evidence against the Assessment Criteria and issues the Designation credential under Section 7.1. The Certificator shall not be the organization itself or an affiliate of it (Section 10.6).

9.4 **Validity and attestation.** A Designation is valid for two years. Each year the organization attests to the Certificator that the required staff are still employed and still certified; the Certificator records the attestation with the Office.

9.5 **Cure.** An organization that no longer meets the staff requirement or whose Qualifying Membership lapses has ninety days to cure. After that period the Designation is suspended, and revoked if the cure is not completed within a further ninety days.

9.6 Staff are counted once: an individual's certification supports the Designation of one organization at a time, the one that employs them.

## SECTION 10. AUTHORIZED CERTIFICATORS

10.1 **Eligibility.** A Certificator holds a Qualifying Membership as an Associate, employs at least two Certified Instructors covering every Track it delivers, operates a verifiable service on a Verana network that holds its Certificator credential, has the capability to deliver training and keep candidate records, and has signed the Authorized Certificator Agreement.

10.2 **Accreditation.** Applications are submitted on veranafoundation.org. The Office reviews an application within two weeks of a complete file, onboards the applicant, issues the Certificator credential and lists the Certificator in the Registry. Accreditation is granted for one year and renewed after the annual review of Section 10.7. The Office may limit an accreditation to specific Tracks, regions or languages.

10.3 **Launch Certificators.** The Office may accredit the first Certificators directly, and certifies their instructors and their organizations itself. The Foundation issues those credentials under Section 7.1.

10.4 **Obligations.** A Certificator shall:

(a) deliver the published Curriculum unchanged, additions being allowed when marked as the Certificator's own;

(b) proctor assessments and grade practical work with Certified Instructors only, against the Assessment Criteria, using the Marker Pack;

(c) report assessment results to the Office within five business days;

(d) keep candidate and assessment records for the validity period of the certifications it issued;

(e) accept audits, including attendance of Office observers and assessments by unannounced candidates;

(f) keep the Marker Pack confidential and use it only for the Program;

(g) use the Marks only as the trademark policy of Section 11 allows;

(h) pay the fees of Annex A;

(i) process candidate personal data in accordance with Section 13;

(j) inform the Office of any change in its instructors, its membership status or its verifiable service.

10.5 **Independence of results.** A Certificator decides on practical and case-study results independently and in good faith. The Office may overturn a reported result by opening a review within the ten business days Section 7.1 gives it to record the result. A result already issued is challenged under Section 8.

10.6 **Conflicts of interest.** A Certificator shall not certify its own organization, its affiliates, or its own employees and contractors, nor grade their work. Such certifications are delivered by the Office or by another Certificator. Where the Office delivers them, the Foundation issues the credential under Section 7.1.

10.7 **Annual review.** Each year the Office reviews the Certificator's results, audit findings, candidate feedback and instructor coverage, and renews, limits or ends the accreditation.

10.8 **Suspension and withdrawal.** The Office may suspend an accreditation with immediate effect for a breach of Section 5.6, Section 10.4 or Section 10.6, for loss of its Qualifying Membership, or for conduct that brings the Program into disrepute, and may withdraw it after hearing the Certificator. On withdrawal, the Certificator's credential and its issuer entries in the Verana Certification Ecosystem are revoked, it stops using the Marks, and it hands its candidate records to the Office.

## SECTION 11. THE MARKS

11.1 Until the Verana Foundation accepts assignment of the Program in writing, the Marks are held by 2060 OÜ in its own name. From that acceptance, the Marks belong to the Verana Foundation. Holders of a valid certification and accredited Certificators receive a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to use the Mark that corresponds to their certification or accreditation, in the form published by the Foundation, for the duration of its validity.

11.2 A Mark may be used only with the name of its holder and only for the Track, Designation or accreditation actually held. A Mark shall not be used in a way that suggests endorsement by the Foundation of a product, a service or an opinion, nor altered, combined with another mark or registered as a domain name or company name.

11.3 The license ends with the certification or accreditation. The Foundation may require the removal of a Mark used in breach of this Section.

## SECTION 12. APPEALS AND COMPLAINTS

12.1 A Candidate, a holder or a Certificator may appeal a decision of the Office or a result within thirty days of its notification, in writing, stating the grounds.

12.2 The Office decides the appeal within thirty days after hearing the appellant and, where relevant, the Certificator. A second appeal lies to the Foundation, whose decision is final within the Program. On an appeal of a result, the Office gives the appellant the Assessment Criteria applied to the work and the points awarded against each criterion, and does not disclose the Marker Pack.

12.3 Complaints about a Certificator are addressed to the Office, which investigates and may audit the Certificator.

12.4 Appeals and complaints do not suspend the contested decision unless the Office decides otherwise.

## SECTION 13. DATA PROTECTION AND RECORDS

13.1 The Foundation processes the personal data of Candidates and holders to administer enrollments, assessments, certifications and the Registry, as described in the privacy policy of veranafoundation.org. Assessment records, including attempts, scores and proctoring records, are kept for the validity period of the related certification and twenty-four months after it.

13.2 A Certificator processes the personal data of its Candidates for its workshops and assessments under its own responsibility and in accordance with applicable data protection law, and shares with the Office only what the Program requires.

13.3 Publication in the Registry of a Certified Professional requires the holder's consent and may be withdrawn at any time; Certified Organizations and Certificators are published as a condition of the Program.

13.4 Credentials issued on a Verana network contain the claims of Section 7.2 and no other personal data. A revoked or expired credential remains verifiable as revoked or expired.

## SECTION 14. FEES

14.1 The fees payable under the Program are set out in Annex A. A fee is consideration for accreditation, for issuing a credential, or for an exam attempt. It is not a membership due, and Section 7.7 of the Membership Agreement does not describe it.

14.2 Until the Verana Foundation accepts assignment of the Program in writing, 2060 OÜ invoices these fees in its own name. From that acceptance, the Foundation invoices them. Accreditation fees and issuance fees are invoiced as Annex A provides. Membership dues are invoiced separately under the Membership Agreement. An exam fee is invoiced to the Candidate alone. The membership dues characterisation is not sent with a Program invoice.

14.3 Sections 7.9(a), 7.9(b), 7.9(d), 7.10, and 7.11(a), (b), and (d) of the Membership Agreement apply to the payment of these fees. The payer stands in the place of the Member, and these fees stand in the place of membership fees, for those provisions only. The assistance mentioned in Section 7.10 does not include a statement under Section 7.11(c). Sections 7.7, 7.9(c), and 7.11(c) do not apply.

14.4 The fees are exclusive of value added tax and similar taxes. Where the Certificator is a taxable person outside Estonia, the accreditation fee and an issuance fee charged to the Certificator are a business supply and may fall under a reverse charge. An issuance fee invoiced to an applicant under Section A.2 is a business supply where that applicant is a taxable person outside Estonia, and may fall under a reverse charge, and is a consumer supply where that applicant is an individual. An exam fee charged to a Candidate is a consumer supply. The rate and the place of supply follow the law that applies to that supply.

14.5 Certificators set the prices of their own workshops, coaching and services freely and invoice them directly.

14.6 Fees are non-refundable once an assessment has been attempted or a credential issued, except as required by law.

## SECTION 15. VERSIONS, AMENDMENTS AND PUBLICATION

15.1 This Policy is versioned. Each version is published on veranafoundation.org with its integrity digest, and the digest of the version in force is anchored as the governance framework of the Verana Certification Ecosystem.

15.2 The Foundation may amend the Policy. An amendment takes effect on publication of the new version, with at least thirty days' notice to Certificators for changes to Section 10 or to Annex A, except for changes required by law.

15.3 An assessment is governed by the Program Version in force on the day of the attempt; a certification is governed by the Policy version in force on the day of issuance, and by later versions for its renewal, suspension and revocation.

## SECTION 16. LIABILITY

16.1 A certification attests that the holder met the requirements of the Program at the time of assessment. It is not a warranty of the holder's future performance, of any product or service, or of compliance with any law.

16.2 The Foundation and the Certificators are not liable to any third party for reliance on a certification. Liability between the Foundation and a Certificator is governed by the Authorized Certificator Agreement; liability between the Foundation and a Candidate by the Candidate Terms.
