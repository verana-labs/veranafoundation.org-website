2060 and the Certificator are hereinafter referred to individually as a **"Party"** and collectively as the **"Parties."**

## RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, 2060 operates the Verana Certification Program (the **"Program"**) under the Verana Certification Policy (the **"Policy"**), through which individuals and organizations are certified in the use, sale, construction and operation of products and services based on the Verana specifications and software;

**WHEREAS**, the Certificator holds a Qualifying Membership as an Associate under the Policy, pursuant to a Membership Agreement dated {{membership_effective_date}} (the **"Membership Agreement"**), and wishes to deliver training, assess candidates and issue certifications under the Program;

**WHEREAS**, 2060 has reviewed the Certificator's application under Section 10 of the Policy and is willing to accredit the Certificator on the terms of this Agreement;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein, the Parties agree as follows.

## SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS AND PRECEDENCE

1.1 Capitalized terms used and not defined in this Agreement have the meaning given in the Policy and, failing that, in the Membership Agreement.

1.2 The Policy, in its version in force from time to time, is incorporated into this Agreement by reference. In case of conflict, this Agreement prevails over the Policy, and the Membership Agreement prevails over both on the matters it governs (membership, intellectual property and licensing of contributions, code of conduct, governing law and dispute resolution).

1.3 **"Foundation"** means 2060 until the Verana Foundation accepts an assignment of this Agreement in writing under Section 15.3, and means the Verana Foundation from that acceptance. Every section of this Agreement binds the Parties on the Effective Date. Section 2 of the Membership Agreement (Hybrid Binding Structure) does not apply to this Agreement. Section 1.2 does not allow that Section 2 to defer, limit, or condition any section of this Agreement.

## SECTION 2. ACCREDITATION

2.1 **Grant.** The Foundation accredits the Certificator as a Verana Authorized Certificator for the Tracks, regions and languages listed in Annex A, and grants the Certificator the non-exclusive right to deliver training, proctor assessments, grade practical work and issue certifications under the Program within that scope.

2.2 **Non-exclusivity.** The Foundation may accredit any number of Certificators and may itself certify candidates and organizations.

2.3 **Scope changes.** The Certificator may request an extension of Annex A. The Foundation may limit Annex A on renewal or under Section 13.

2.4 **No agency.** The Certificator is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an agency, partnership, joint venture, employment or franchise relationship. The Certificator shall not bind the Foundation or hold itself out as the Foundation.

## SECTION 3. TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 This Agreement enters into force on the Effective Date and runs for one year.

3.2 It renews for successive one-year periods upon the annual review of Section 10.7 of the Policy, unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty days before the end of the current period, or the Foundation decides not to renew following the review.

3.3 Renewal is conditional on the Certificator holding a Qualifying Membership as an Associate for the whole of the renewed period.

## SECTION 4. OBLIGATIONS OF THE CERTIFICATOR

4.1 **Curriculum fidelity.** The Certificator shall deliver the Curriculum of the applicable Program Version as published by the Foundation. The Certificator may add its own material when it is clearly marked as such and does not alter the learning objectives, the assessment or the pass criteria.

4.2 **Instructors.** The Certificator shall employ or contract at least two Certified Instructors covering every Track in Annex A, shall notify the Foundation of every change within ten business days, and shall ensure that only Certified Instructors proctor assessments and grade practical work.

4.3 **Assessment integrity.** The Certificator shall proctor assessments in accordance with the Policy and the candidate-facing rules in the Assessment Criteria, shall grade against the Assessment Criteria, shall use the Marker Pack only for proctoring and grading, shall not disclose the Marker Pack to a Candidate, shall grade independently and in good faith, and shall report every assessment result to the Foundation within five business days.

4.4 **Records.** The Certificator shall keep enrollment, attendance, assessment and grading records for every Candidate for the validity period of the related certification and twenty-four months after it, and shall make them available to the Foundation on request.

4.5 **Verifiable service.** The Certificator shall operate a verifiable service on the Verana network designated by the Policy, holding its Verana Authorized Certificator credential and its issuer accreditations in the Verana Certification Ecosystem, and shall issue the credentials of Section 7 from that service.

4.6 **Conflicts of interest.** The Certificator shall not certify, assess or grade its own organization, its affiliates, or its own employees and contractors, and shall disclose to the Foundation any other circumstance that could reasonably be seen to compromise the independence of a result.

4.7 **Audits.** The Certificator shall allow the Foundation, on reasonable notice, to observe training and assessment sessions, to review records, to interview instructors and candidates, and to enroll unannounced candidates. The Certificator shall remedy audit findings within the period set by the Foundation.

4.8 **Candidate relations.** The Certificator shall provide every Candidate with the Candidate Terms before the first assessment, shall handle candidate complaints promptly and shall forward to the Foundation any appeal under Section 12 of the Policy within five business days.

4.9 **Conduct.** The Certificator shall comply with the Code of Conduct annexed to the Membership Agreement in all Program activities, and shall not make any statement that misrepresents the Program, the Foundation or the meaning of a certification.

4.10 **Notices.** The Certificator shall inform the Foundation without delay of any change in its legal form, control, membership status, verifiable service or instructor coverage, of any security incident affecting candidate data or assessment content, and of any claim relating to the Program.

## SECTION 5. OBLIGATIONS OF THE FOUNDATION

5.1 The Foundation shall publish and maintain the Curriculum, including the Assessment Criteria, and shall give the Certificator access to the Marker Pack needed to deliver the Program within Annex A.

5.2 The Foundation shall operate the Program platform on veranafoundation.org, administer knowledge exams, maintain the Registry, and process reported results within ten business days.

5.3 The Foundation shall list the Certificator in the Registry and on veranafoundation.org with its scope under Annex A, and shall refer enquiries from prospective candidates and organizations to the listed Certificators without preference.

5.4 The Foundation shall give at least thirty days' notice of a new Program Version, of a change to the Policy that affects Section 10 or Annex A of the Policy, and of any change to the fee schedule.

5.5 The Foundation shall decide applications, appeals and audit findings in accordance with the Policy and shall notify its decisions to the Certificator in writing.

## SECTION 6. FEES

6.1 The Certificator shall pay the Foundation the fees set out in Annex A of the Policy as in force from time to time: the annual accreditation fee, invoiced on the Effective Date and at each renewal, and the issuance fees, invoiced monthly in arrears on the basis of the credentials issued in the month. These fees are consideration for accreditation and for issuing credentials. They are not membership dues.

6.2 The accreditation fee is in addition to the Associate membership dues under the Membership Agreement.

6.3 Invoices under this Section 6 are issued by 2060 in its own name until the Verana Foundation accepts the assignment under Section 15.3, and by the Verana Foundation from that acceptance. They are payable within thirty days of the invoice date. An invoice under this Section 6 does not include Associate membership dues and is not accompanied by the membership dues characterisation. Sections 7.9(a), 7.9(b), 7.9(d), 7.10, and 7.11(a), (b), and (d) of the Membership Agreement apply to every payment under this Section 6, the Certificator standing in the place of the Member and these fees standing in the place of membership fees. The assistance mentioned in Section 7.10 does not include a statement under Section 7.11(c). Sections 7.7, 7.9(c), and 7.11(c) do not apply.

6.4 Fees paid are non-refundable, including on early termination, except as required by law.

6.5 The Certificator sets the prices of its own workshops, coaching and services freely and invoices them directly to its customers. The Foundation is not a party to those contracts.

## SECTION 7. CREDENTIALS AND THE REGISTRY

7.1 The Foundation issues the Verana Authorized Certificator credential to the Certificator's DID and onboards the Certificator as an issuer of the Verana Certified Professional and Verana Certified Organization credential schemas in the Verana Certification Ecosystem, within the scope of Annex A.

7.2 The Certificator issues Verana Certified Professional credentials to the individuals it has assessed and Verana Certified Organization credentials to the organizations it has reviewed, only after the Foundation has confirmed the assessment results, and with the claims defined by the Foundation and no others.

7.3 The Certificator shall revoke a credential it issued when instructed by the Foundation under Section 8 of the Policy, within five business days.

7.4 The Foundation may at any time re-attest, suspend or revoke any credential issued under the Program, and may suspend or revoke the Certificator's own credential and issuer accreditations under Section 13.

7.5 When the Program moves to the Verana main network under Section 7.3 of the Policy, the Certificator shall re-issue, at no charge to holders, the valid credentials it issued, within the period set by the Foundation.

## SECTION 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND MARKS

8.1 **Curriculum.** The published Curriculum, including the Assessment Criteria, is licensed to the public under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license. The Certificator may use, translate and adapt it under that license, keeping the attribution to the Verana Foundation, and shall mark its adaptations as its own under Section 4.1. The Marker Pack is not part of the Curriculum and is not licensed under this Section 8.1.

8.2 **Marker Pack.** The Marker Pack is licensed to the Certificator only for the delivery of the Program during the term, is not sublicensable, and remains the property of the Foundation or its licensors. The Certificator shall not disclose it to a Candidate or to any person who does not need it to proctor or grade.

8.3 **Contributions.** Material that the Certificator contributes to the Curriculum through the Showcase and Certification Working Group is contributed under Section 4 of the Membership Agreement.

8.4 **Marks.** The Foundation grants the Certificator, for the term and within Annex A, a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, revocable license to use the "Verana Authorized Certificator" mark and the Program marks in the forms published by the Foundation, in accordance with Section 11 of the Policy and the Foundation's trademark guidelines. The Certificator acquires no right in the Marks, shall not challenge them, and shall stop using them on termination.

8.5 **Certificator's own marks.** The Certificator grants the Foundation a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use the Certificator's name and logo to list it in the Registry and on veranafoundation.org for the term.

## SECTION 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Section 5 of the Membership Agreement applies to this Agreement. The material of Section 8.2, assessment results before their notification to the Candidate, audit findings, and the personal data of Candidates are Confidential Information of the Foundation; the Certificator's candidate lists and commercial terms are Confidential Information of the Certificator.

9.2 The Certificator shall protect the Marker Pack with at least the same care as its own most sensitive information, shall restrict access to Certified Instructors and staff who need it, and shall report any suspected disclosure within twenty-four hours.

## SECTION 10. DATA PROTECTION

10.1 Each Party processes the personal data of Candidates as an independent controller for its own purposes: the Certificator for its workshops, enrollments and assessments, the Foundation for the administration of the Program and the Registry.

10.2 The Certificator shall process candidate personal data in accordance with applicable data protection law, inform Candidates of the transfer of their results to the Foundation, and transmit to the Foundation only the data the Program requires: identity, contact details, enrollment, attendance and assessment results.

10.3 Each Party shall assist the other, within reason, in answering requests of data subjects and authorities relating to the Program.

10.4 Where the Parties later determine that a processing is carried out jointly or on behalf of the other, they shall enter into the arrangement that the law requires.

## SECTION 11. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

11.1 Each Party represents that it is validly existing, has the authority to enter into this Agreement, and that this Agreement does not conflict with any obligation binding on it.

11.2 The Certificator represents that it holds a Qualifying Membership as an Associate, that the information in its application is accurate and complete, that it has the instructors, facilities and processes stated in its application, and that it is not subject to sanctions, insolvency proceedings or a conviction that would bring the Program into disrepute.

11.3 Except as expressly stated, the Curriculum, the platform and the credentials are provided as they are, without warranty of any kind, and the Foundation gives no warranty as to the commercial results the Certificator may obtain.

## SECTION 12. LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

12.1 Section 9 of the Membership Agreement (Limitation of Liability) applies to this Agreement, with the exception that the aggregate liability of 2060 and of the Verana Foundation under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by the Certificator under Section 6 in the twelve months preceding the claim.

12.2 The Certificator shall indemnify and hold harmless 2060, the Verana Foundation, and their officers against any third-party claim arising from the Certificator's training, assessment, grading, use of the Marks, processing of personal data, or breach of this Agreement, except to the extent caused by the Foundation.

12.3 A certification issued under the Program attests the result of an assessment at a point in time and is not a warranty by either Party of the holder's future performance.

## SECTION 13. SUSPENSION AND TERMINATION

13.1 **Termination for convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with ninety days' written notice.

13.2 **Suspension.** The Foundation may suspend the accreditation with immediate effect, in whole or for part of Annex A, on the grounds of Section 10.8 of the Policy, pending a decision under Section 13.3. During a suspension the Certificator shall not enroll new Candidates, proctor assessments or issue credentials, and shall say so to its Candidates.

13.3 **Termination for cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect if the other Party materially breaches it and, where the breach can be cured, does not cure it within thirty days of notice. The Foundation may terminate with immediate effect for a breach of Section 4.3, 4.6, 9 or 10, for loss or lapse of the Certificator's Qualifying Membership as an Associate, for insolvency, or for conduct that brings the Program into disrepute.

13.4 **Automatic termination.** This Agreement terminates automatically when the Membership Agreement terminates.

13.5 **Hearing.** Before terminating for cause the Foundation shall hear the Certificator, unless the breach is such that a hearing would be futile or harmful to Candidates.

13.6 **Effects.** On termination for any reason the Certificator shall stop using the Marks and presenting itself as a Certificator; the Foundation revokes the Certificator's credential and issuer accreditations; the Certificator completes, within thirty days, the assessments already started with the Foundation's cooperation, or transfers the Candidates to the Foundation or to another Certificator; the Certificator hands its candidate records to the Foundation and deletes the Marker Pack; and accrued fees remain due. Certifications issued before termination remain valid under Section 8.3 of the Policy. Sections 6 (to the extent of outstanding obligations), 8.2, 8.4 (last sentence), 9, 10, 12, 13.6, 14 and 15 survive.

## SECTION 14. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

14.1 Sections 10 (Governing Law) and 11 (Dispute Resolution) of the Membership Agreement apply to this Agreement as if set out in full, the Certificator standing in the place of the Member.

14.2 Decisions of the Certification Office on assessments, accreditation scope, audits and appeals are decisions within the Program and are subject first to the appeal procedure of Section 12 of the Policy.

## SECTION 15. MISCELLANEOUS

15.1 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, the Policy and the Membership Agreement are the entire agreement between the Parties on the Program and supersede any prior discussion.

15.2 **Amendments.** Amendments to this Agreement require a written document signed by both Parties. Amendments to the Policy and to its fee schedule are made under Section 15 of the Policy and bind the Certificator on notice; a Certificator that does not accept an amendment to Section 10 or Annex A of the Policy may terminate this Agreement under Section 13.1 before the amendment takes effect, in which case the ninety-day notice is reduced to the notice period of the amendment.

15.3 **Assignment.** The Certificator may not assign or transfer this Agreement, including by change of control, without the Foundation's written consent. 2060 shall assign this Agreement to the Verana Foundation upon its incorporation, and the Certificator consents to that assignment now. The assignment takes effect when the Verana Foundation accepts it in writing. Until that acceptance, 2060 remains the obligor under every section. From that acceptance, the Verana Foundation assumes the rights and obligations under this Agreement. 2060 is released from obligations that arise after the acceptance and remains liable for obligations that accrued before it.

15.4 **Notices.** Notices are given in writing by email to the addresses below, with effect on the business day following transmission, unless a bounce is received.

15.5 **Severability, waiver, counterparts, language.** Sections 12.1 to 12.5 of the Membership Agreement apply. This Agreement is executed in English; translations are for convenience only.
