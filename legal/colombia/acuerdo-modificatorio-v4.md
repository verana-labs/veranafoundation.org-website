## RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, the Parties entered into the Verana Foundation Membership Agreement, version v4 dated 24 June 2026, executed by the Member on {{agreement_date}} (the **“Agreement”**);

**WHEREAS**, the Foundation (in Formation) has adopted version v5 of the Membership Agreement, dated 9 October 2026, which clarifies the nature of membership fees, identifies the Invoicing Entity, and sets out the treatment of taxes and withholding, currency and payment charges, and tax documentation;

**WHEREAS**, the Parties wish to incorporate those provisions into the Agreement by written amendment executed by both Parties in accordance with Section 12.2 of the Agreement;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the Parties agree as follows:

**1. Amendments.**

1.1 Section 1 (Definitions) of the Agreement is amended by adding Section 1.29 (“Invoicing Entity”) as set out in Annex 1 to this Amendment.

1.2 Section 7.2 (Obligation to Pay Fees) of the Agreement is deleted and replaced by the text of Section 7.2 set out in Annex 1.

1.3 Sections 7.7 (Nature of Membership Dues), 7.8 (Invoicing Entity; Payment Instructions), 7.9 (Taxes; No Deduction; Gross-Up), 7.10 (Currency, Payment Methods, and Charges), and 7.11 (Tax Documentation and Cooperation), as set out in Annex 1, are inserted after Section 7.6 of the Agreement. The former Section 7.7 (Survival) is renumbered Section 7.12 without change to its text.

1.4 Annex D (Fee Schedule) of the Agreement is amended by inserting Sections D.8 (Invoicing and Payment) and D.9 (Nature of Dues; Taxes), as set out in Annex 1, after Section D.7. The former Sections D.8 (Effective Date and Amendments) and D.9 (Survival) are renumbered D.10 and D.11 without change to their text.

**2. Effect.** This Amendment takes effect on the Amendment Effective Date and applies to all membership fees invoiced, or outstanding, on or after that date. The provisions inserted by this Amendment form part of Section 7 and Annex D of the Agreement and are Immediate Binding Provisions within the meaning of Section 2.1 of the Agreement.

**3. No Other Change.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement. References in the Agreement to “this Agreement” are to the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

**4. Governing Law; Disputes; Miscellaneous.** Sections 10 (Governing Law), 11 (Dispute Resolution), and 12 (Miscellaneous) of the Agreement apply to this Amendment as if set out in full herein.

**5. Language.** This Amendment is executed in English. The Spanish text below is a courtesy translation provided for the Member’s convenience; in case of discrepancy, the English text prevails.

**Acuerdo Modificatorio No. 1 al Membership Agreement de Verana Foundation.** Entre Verana Foundation (en formación), representada por 2060 OÜ, y {{member_legal_name}}.

Las Partes acuerdan: (1) incorporar al Agreement (versión v4) las disposiciones de la versión v5 relativas a la naturaleza de las cuotas de membresía (Sección 7.7), la entidad facturadora e instrucciones de pago (Secciones 1.29 y 7.8), impuestos, no deducción y *gross-up* (Sección 7.9), moneda, medios de pago y costos (Sección 7.10), documentación tributaria y cooperación (Sección 7.11), y las Secciones D.8 y D.9 del Anexo D, reemplazando la Sección 7.2 y renumerando las secciones existentes; (2) que el Acuerdo Modificatorio rige desde la fecha indicada y aplica a toda cuota facturada o pendiente desde esa fecha, con carácter de disposición de vigencia inmediata (Sección 2.1); (3) que el Agreement permanece sin cambios en todo lo demás; (4) que aplican las Secciones 10, 11 y 12 del Agreement; y (5) que el texto en inglés prevalece sobre esta traducción.

En resumen, las cláusulas incorporadas establecen que las cuotas son cuotas anuales de afiliación a una fundación sin ánimo de lucro y no contraprestación por servicios; que se facturan y pagan en euros a la entidad facturadora (2060 OÜ durante el periodo de formación) con todos los costos bancarios y de conversión a cargo del Miembro; que se pagan sin deducción ni retención alguna y que, si la ley exigiera una retención, el Miembro paga el valor adicional necesario para que la entidad facturadora reciba el valor íntegro y entrega el certificado correspondiente en treinta días; y que la entidad facturadora entrega, a solicitud, certificado de residencia fiscal, extracto del registro mercantil y una declaración sobre la naturaleza de las cuotas.

# **ANNEX 1**

## **TEXT OF THE AMENDED AND INSERTED PROVISIONS**

(Reproduced verbatim from Membership Agreement v5, 20261009.)

**Section 1.29 (added)**

1.29 **“Invoicing Entity”** means the entity that issues invoices for, and collects, membership fees on behalf of the Foundation, being, during the formation period, 2060 OÜ acting in the capacity described in Section 1.15, and thereafter the Foundation itself or such other entity as the Foundation may designate by written notice to the Member pursuant to Section 7.8.

**Section 7.2 (replaced)**

7.2 **Obligation to Pay Fees**. The Member hereby covenants and agrees to pay all membership fees assessed pursuant to the Fee Schedule in full, without offset, deduction, counterclaim, or withholding of any kind, subject only to Section 7.9 (Taxes; No Deduction; Gross-Up). The Member further agrees to pay all such fees by the deadlines set forth in the Fee Schedule or otherwise established by the Foundation. For the avoidance of doubt, payment of fees is a condition precedent to the continuation of the Member’s rights and privileges under this Agreement.

**Sections 7.7 to 7.12 (inserted; former 7.7 renumbered 7.12)**

7.7 **Nature of Membership Dues**. The Parties acknowledge and agree that membership fees are annual membership dues paid in consideration of admission to, and continued standing in, a Membership Class of a non-profit foundation, and that they constitute a contribution to the general funding of the Foundation’s mission. Membership fees (a) are assessed solely by reference to the Member’s Membership Class and the dues tier set out in the Fee Schedule, and not by reference to any use, consumption, or volume of any Foundation activity, resource, or benefit; (b) are not consideration for the rendering of any service to the Member, and in particular are not consideration for technical services, technical assistance, consulting or advisory services, software, the licensing or use of any intellectual property, or access to or use of any platform, network, or infrastructure; (c) are not conditioned upon, and do not vary with, the Member’s participation or non-participation in Working Groups or other Foundation activities, which participation is voluntary and open on identical terms to all Members of the relevant Membership Class; and (d) are non-refundable in accordance with Section 7.3. The Parties note that Contributor Members, who enjoy participation rights under Annex B, pay no membership fees, which confirms that membership fees are not the price of participation rights. No person acting for the Foundation or the Invoicing Entity renders any service to the Member in the Member’s jurisdiction in connection with the membership fees. The Parties intend that membership fees be characterized for all purposes, including accounting and tax purposes, consistently with this Section 7.7.

7.8 **Invoicing Entity; Payment Instructions**. Membership fees shall be invoiced by, and paid to, the Invoicing Entity. During the formation period, the Invoicing Entity is 2060 OÜ, which issues invoices and receives membership fees exclusively for the account of the Foundation (in Formation) and applies them to the Foundation’s purposes. Upon incorporation, the Foundation may, by written notice to the Member, designate itself or any other entity as Invoicing Entity, and may change the Invoicing Entity or its payment details from time to time by written notice. Such notice shall not constitute an amendment of this Agreement. The Member shall pay only to the account or payment method stated in the invoice or in a written notice given in accordance with Section 12.4, and shall verify any change of beneficiary or bank details through the Foundation’s published contact channels before making payment. Payment made to any other account shall not discharge the Member’s payment obligation.

7.9 **Taxes; No Deduction; Gross-Up**.

(a) All membership fees are stated exclusive of value added tax, sales tax, goods and services tax, consumption tax, and any similar indirect tax. Where applicable law requires the Member to self-assess or account for any such tax on the membership fees under a reverse-charge or similar mechanism, the Member shall do so at its own cost, and no such tax shall reduce the amount payable to the Invoicing Entity.

(b) All membership fees shall be paid free and clear of, and without any deduction or withholding on account of, any tax, levy, duty, or charge of any nature. If the Member is required by applicable law to deduct or withhold any amount from a payment of membership fees, the Member shall (i) pay to the Invoicing Entity such additional amount as is necessary to ensure that the Invoicing Entity receives, free of any deduction or withholding, the full amount it would have received had no deduction or withholding been required; (ii) pay the amount deducted or withheld to the competent authority within the time allowed by law; and (iii) deliver to the Invoicing Entity, within thirty (30) days of such payment, an official receipt or certificate evidencing it.

(c) The Member is solely responsible for determining the tax treatment of the membership fees under the laws of its jurisdiction and shall make that determination in good faith, having regard to Section 7.7 and to the documentation provided under Section 7.11. Nothing in this Agreement constitutes tax advice by the Foundation or the Invoicing Entity.

(d) Each Party shall bear its own income, profit, and similar taxes.

7.10 **Currency, Payment Methods, and Charges**. Membership fees are denominated, invoiced, and payable in euro (EUR) unless the Fee Schedule or the invoice states otherwise. The Member shall pay by one of the payment methods stated in the invoice, which may include payment by card through the Foundation’s payment processor and payment by bank transfer. All bank charges, correspondent and intermediary bank fees, currency conversion costs, payment processor fees charged to the payer, and any other costs of transmitting payment shall be borne by the Member, so that the Invoicing Entity receives the full invoiced amount in the invoiced currency. A payment is made when the full invoiced amount is credited, without reservation, to the account of the Invoicing Entity. The Member is responsible for complying with any exchange-control, foreign-payment reporting, or similar requirements applicable in its jurisdiction, and the Invoicing Entity shall provide reasonable assistance under Section 7.11. The Member accepts invoices issued and delivered electronically, without signature.

7.11 **Tax Documentation and Cooperation**. Upon the Member’s reasonable written request, the Invoicing Entity shall provide, at no charge, (a) a certificate of its tax residence issued by the competent authority of its jurisdiction; (b) an extract of its commercial register; (c) a written statement confirming the nature of the membership fees consistently with Section 7.7 and confirming, where that is the case, that the Invoicing Entity has no branch, office, personnel, or fixed place of business in the Member’s jurisdiction; and (d) such other information as is reasonably necessary for the Member to comply with its tax, accounting, and exchange-control obligations. The Member shall provide the Invoicing Entity with its tax identification number and billing address and, where a deduction or withholding has been made notwithstanding Section 7.9, the receipts or certificates required under Section 7.9(b)(iii). The Parties shall cooperate in good faith to lawfully minimize any tax, deduction, or withholding on the membership fees.

7.12 **Survival**. The Member’s obligations to pay any outstanding fees, reimbursements, charges, or other financial obligations accrued prior to the termination of this Agreement shall survive such termination until fully satisfied.

**Annex D, Sections D.8 to D.11 (D.8 and D.9 inserted; former D.8 and D.9 renumbered D.10 and D.11)**

D.8 **Invoicing and Payment.**

Annual dues are invoiced by the Invoicing Entity (Section 7.8) in euro (EUR) upon admission or provisional admission and thereafter annually, and are payable in full within thirty (30) days of the invoice date unless the invoice states a different due date. Invoices are issued electronically and state the payment methods available, which may include card payment through the Foundation’s payment processor and bank transfer. Sections 7.9 (Taxes; No Deduction; Gross-Up), 7.10 (Currency, Payment Methods, and Charges), and 7.11 (Tax Documentation and Cooperation) of the Membership Agreement apply to every payment under this Fee Schedule.

D.9 **Nature of Dues; Taxes.**

Annual dues are membership dues within the meaning of Section 7.7 of the Membership Agreement and are not a charge for services. They are stated exclusive of any value added or similar tax and are payable without deduction or withholding in accordance with Section 7.9.

D.10 **Effective Date and Amendments.**

This Fee Schedule shall take effect upon its adoption by the Foundation (in Formation) and shall remain effective unless and until amended by the Foundation (in Formation) or, upon incorporation, the Foundation. Any amendment shall become effective immediately upon publication or written notice and shall be binding on all Members without need for further consent.

D.11 **Survival.**

The Member’s obligation to pay any annual dues that have accrued prior to termination of membership shall survive termination of the Membership Agreement and shall remain enforceable until fully satisfied.
