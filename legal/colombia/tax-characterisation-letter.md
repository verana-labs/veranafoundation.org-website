Further to the admission of {{member_legal_name}} as an Associate Member of Verana Foundation (in formation), and to facilitate the accounting, tax and exchange-control treatment of the payment of its annual dues, we record the following facts.

**1. Who issues the invoice, and in what capacity**

1.1 2060 OÜ is a private limited company (*osaühing*) incorporated and existing under the laws of the Republic of Estonia, registered in the Estonian commercial register under number 16853041, with its seat at Ahtri tn 12, 15551 Tallinn, Estonia. It is tax resident in Estonia, as evidenced by the certificate of tax residence issued by the Estonian Tax and Customs Board (Maksu- ja Tolliamet) attached to this letter.

1.2 2060 OÜ acts as organiser and promoter of Verana Foundation (in formation), a non-profit foundation whose incorporation is in progress, in the terms of Section 1.15 of the Membership Agreement you signed on {{agreement_date}} (the "Agreement"). In that capacity 2060 OÜ issues the invoices and receives the membership dues exclusively for the account of the Foundation (in formation) and applies them to its purposes (Section 7.8 of the Agreement).

1.3 2060 OÜ has no branch, agency, office, permanent establishment, personnel, representative or fixed place of business in Colombia.

**2. Nature of the payment**

2.1 The invoiced amount is the **annual membership due** for the admission and continued standing of {{member_legal_name}} in the "Associate Member" class of the Foundation, under Section 7 and Annex D (Fee Schedule) of the Agreement.

2.2 The due is a fixed annual amount determined solely by the member's employee-count tier ({{tier_label}}) and identical for every member of that tier. It does not depend on the use, consumption or volume of any activity, resource or benefit of the Foundation, nor on whether the member takes part in working groups or other activities, which are voluntary.

2.3 The due is **not consideration for any service**. In particular it does not remunerate technical services, technical assistance, consulting or advisory services; it does not include any licence, assignment or use of software or intellectual property; and it does not include access to or use of any platform, network or infrastructure (Section 7.7 of the Agreement). Members of the "Contributor Member" class, who enjoy the participation rights of Annex B, pay no dues at all, which confirms that the due is not the price of participation rights.

2.4 The due is non-refundable (Section 7.3 of the Agreement) and is a contribution to the general funding of the Foundation's mission: the stewardship of open specifications, schemas and open-source software for verifiable trust infrastructure.

2.5 All activities of the Foundation (in formation) are administered from Estonia and take place online. No person acting for the Foundation or for 2060 OÜ renders any service in Colombian territory in connection with the due.

**3. Tax treatment in Colombia: our understanding**

3.1 Under articles 12 and 20 of the Colombian Tax Code (Estatuto Tributario), foreign companies without domicile in Colombia are taxpayers only in respect of their Colombian-source income. Article 24 defines Colombian-source income, which includes services rendered inside the national territory and, irrespective of where they are rendered, technical services, technical assistance and consulting.

3.2 The membership due is none of those. It is not Colombian-source income and is therefore not subject to income-tax withholding, since article 406 of the Tax Code orders withholding only on "income subject to tax in Colombia". This is the position of the DIAN Legal Directorate on payments abroad for memberships in foreign organisations, stated in **Oficio 007242 of 30 March 2017** (file 100202208-0276), under which such payments "are neither Colombian-source income nor income constituting taxable income" for the recipient organisation, and on services performed entirely abroad in Oficio 057318 of 2014.

3.3 For the same reason the VAT withholding of article 437-2, paragraph 3, of the Tax Code does not apply, since it presupposes the rendering of taxable services in the national territory. Oficio 007242 of 2017 reached the same conclusion: "there is no basis for withholding on account of sales tax".

3.4 There is no double-taxation treaty between Colombia and Estonia. The treatment described rests exclusively on Colombian domestic law, so no treaty-relief form is required.

3.5 Estonia is not on the list of non-cooperative, low-tax or no-tax jurisdictions in article 1.2.2.5.1 of Decree 1625 of 2016 (Decree 1966 of 2014).

3.6 The above states facts and our good-faith understanding. It is not Colombian tax advice. We recommend that you confirm it with your accountant or tax advisor, who is responsible for determining the treatment applicable to {{member_legal_name}} (Section 7.9(c) of the Agreement).

Bank, intermediary and currency-conversion charges are borne by the member, so that 2060 OÜ receives the full invoiced amount (Section 7.10 of the Agreement).

4.2 If, notwithstanding the above, your organisation concludes that it must apply any withholding, please inform us before paying. Section 7.9 of the Agreement then applies: the member pays the additional amount needed for 2060 OÜ to receive the full invoiced amount and delivers the withholding certificate within thirty (30) days.
